Creamy Keys sound packs

The key sounds in this folder come from Mechvibes (https://github.com/hainguyents13/mechvibes)
and its bundled sound packs:

  nk-cream                 "NK Cream (original by Ryan)" (Mechvibes pack)
  cherrymx-blue-abs        "CherryMX Blue - ABS keycaps" (Mechvibes pack)
  topre-purple-hybrid-pbt  "Topre Purple Hybrid - PBT keycaps" (Mechvibes pack)
  holy-pandas              "pandas" (Mechvibes pack; the content comes from
                           kbsim by Thomas Lai, https://github.com/tplai/kbsim)

Changes made for Corz Client: the Holy Pandas clips were converted from MP3 to WAV,
and three NK Cream files were renamed ("[.wav" -> lbracket.wav, "].wav" -> rbracket.wav,
"caps lock.wav" -> caps_lock.wav) with config.json updated to match. Audio is otherwise unchanged.
